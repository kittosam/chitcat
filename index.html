FAQ

How do bookings work?

Send your dates and postcode. We’ll confirm availability and your estimate. If you’re happy to go ahead, we’ll reserve your dates and arrange a free meet & greet before the first visit to run through routines, meds and keys.

No deposit required. Full payment is due 24 hours before the first visit (or immediately if your booking starts within 48 hours). Bank holidays & peak dates carry a +20% surcharge.

Do you hold keys?

Yes, keys are stored securely off-site with coded tags (no addresses). Key pick-up/return is included (no fee), and we can hold a key for future bookings if you prefer.

What’s your cancellation policy?
  • 7+ days before the first visit: free (full refund / no charge).
  • 24 hours–6 days before: 20% cancellation fee (we refund 80%; or invoice 20% if unpaid).
  • Less than 24 hours before: 50% cancellation fee.
  • After the booking begins, completed visits are non-refundable.

Peak/bank-holiday surcharges are non-refundable once applied.

Are you insured/DBS checked?

Absolutely! Public liability insurance up to £5m (Protectivity, policy #444900301998) and a current DBS.

What does a Care Report include?

After each visit you’ll get a short message with time in/out, food & water, litter, play/enrichment, meds (if any), and 2–4 photos or a quick clip.
