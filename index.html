FAQ

How do bookings work?


Send your dates and postcode. We’ll confirm availability and arrange a free meet & greet. A 25% deposit secures the booking. The balance is due 24 hours before the first visit (or immediately if your booking starts within 48 hours).

Do you hold keys?


Yes, keys are stored securely off-site with coded tags (no addresses). Key pick-up/return is included (no fee), and we can hold a key for future bookings if you prefer.

What’s your cancellation policy?

  • 5+ days before the first visit: full refund (including the deposit).
  • Inside 5 days but at least 24 hours before: we keep the 25% deposit, refund the rest.
  • Within 24 hours of the start or after the booking begins: no refund.

Bank holidays & peak dates carry a 20% surcharge.

Are you insured/DBS checked?


Absolutely! Public liability insurance up to £5m (Protectivity, policy #444900301998) and a current DBS.

What does a Care Report include?


After each visit you’ll get a short message with time in/out, food & water, litter, play/enrichment, meds (if any), and 2–4 photos or a quick clip — all in one place. Consistent, concise, and reliable.
